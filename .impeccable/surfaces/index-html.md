# Homepage surface brief

## Scope and mode
The single-page portfolio at `index.html`. Mode: **Persuade**. A recruiter (web/software or game studio, weighted equally) should end by downloading the CV or emailing.

## Job, proof, constraints
- The first screen shows both tracks with real proof: Iqraa Library (public repo) for web, Pexet Inc (a publicly announced game, linked to its Steam page) for games. Event Management System sits lower, with no repo link.
- Remove the unproven decoration: stat bars, "projects built", the trophy portrait.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS with no build step. Pexet and Thurayya copy follows thurayyastudio.com and the Steam page (title: Co-founder & Game Developer). Arabic copy stays short (name, nav, section heads, ثريا) and needs a native-speaker check.
- Replace the canvas mini-game with the ثريا dot-settle moment.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Wael as a bilingual screenprint poster. Arabic reads in from the right and English from the left, and the two tracks of work meet on one axis. It refuses the dev-portfolio template of a split hero over equal project cards.
OWN-WORLD:
- Four flat inks only, never tinted or blended: night indigo #1B2147, paper #F4F1EA, star-gold #E8C26A, ink #111111. Re-inked after Wael's feedback: saturated ultramarine and vermilion fields were hard on the eyes. Gold marks and accents; it never fills a whole band.
- Each section is one full-bleed ink band, with crop-mark ticks on a 12-column grid and a centre axis.
- Alexandria sets matched Arabic and Latin display; Readex Pro sets the body.
- Projects use ruled spec tables and origin tickets, and contact is a perforated tear-off strip.
- Raises:
  - PC-98: locked inks.
  - Seed packet: promise line, then spec table.
  - Zine: one marked line per band.
  - Oscilloscope: measured grid, axis as zero.
  - Bazaar: origin ticket.
  - Multiplane: banded plates.
STORY: The visitor sees one person working across two tracks (web systems and game code), built from Khobar and from Thurayya Studio. They believe the proof because it links or is honestly labelled, and they take a tab: CV or email.
FIRST VIEWPORT:
- A night-indigo band.
- WAEL QUBAISI (left) and وائل قبيسي (right), each about 7rem, meeting at the centre axis where the seven-star Thurayya cluster (four-point gold stars) sits.
- One marked intro line under the names.
- Two lanes: WEB → Iqraa Library (View code) and ← GAMES Pexet Inc (Wishlist on Steam).
- A perforated tear-off strip at the foot: Download CV (primary), Email, GitHub, LinkedIn.
FORM: Two-Script Poster, #5 on the grounded list (Arabic–Latin bilingual type poster). seed:97ed18a8de61a7aa
SIGNATURE: The Studio band is night sky. Seven gold four-point stars hang in open sky above ثريا, clear of the heading. On scroll, five fly down and turn into the word's letter dots as they land, and two stay in the sky. Reduced motion shows the finished word with the two stars.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
